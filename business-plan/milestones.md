# Milestones

## Phase 1 — Bootstrap (Sep–Dec 2026)

- Form LLC, get EIN, open business bank account
- Get liability insurance quotes; start CORI background check
- Build SAT diagnostic + domain teaching notes + starter item bank
- Draft intake paperwork (service agreement, waiver, payment terms)
- Buy core book set
- Outreach: Boston Central Korean Culture School, BostonKorea.com, bostonktown.com
- Target: first 2–4 students via home visits

## Phase 2 — Referral Engine (Nov 2026)

- Target 6–8 active students via referrals + expanded outreach
- Begin GRE materials build
- Start touring coworking options near Harvard Square (Workbar, Regus)

## Phase 3 — 10 Students, Commit to Space (Dec 2026)

- Reach ~10 active students
- Finalize coworking space choice, flexible/month-to-month terms
- Confirm insurance requirements the space imposes (additional insured, etc.)

## Phase 4 — Soft Launch (Jan 2027)

- Move into space, set up materials/signage
- Finalize all required insurance before students physically arrive
- Transition existing students; continue building pipeline

## Phase 5 — Cambridge Full Launch (Feb 2027)

- Fully operating location live
- Optional open-house event with a community partner
- Reassess unit economics with real rent/utilization data

## Phase 6 — Cambridge Ramp to Full Utilization (through ~Sep 2027)

- Hire tutors 1–4 (~$40/hr, above-market to attract elite-credentialed talent)
- Push toward full daily volume: 16 group-student visits + 4 individual-student
  visits per day, cumulative

## Phase 7 — Newton (Oct 2027 → Jan 2028) and Lexington (Feb 2028 → May 2028)

- Replicate the proven Cambridge playbook in each new town
- Each location ramps on its own local demand curve, not a fixed calendar

## Long-Run Second Product

- Once real session data exists, build the AI diagnostic/curriculum engine and
  license it to non-competing tutoring companies outside this operating footprint
