# Charles River Tutors

AI-informed, community-first SAT / ACT / GRE tutoring, launching in Greater Boston's
Korean-American community before expanding to a licensable curriculum engine for
other tutoring companies.

## Problem Statement

Standardized testing is back as a requirement at nearly every selective school, but
test prep hasn't caught up: families are stuck choosing between expensive, impersonal
group classes that ignore individual weaknesses, or one-off tutors with no system for
tracking what a student actually needs from session to session. This gap is sharpest
in communities where trust, credentials, and word-of-mouth matter more than a website.

## Vision Statement

A future where every student's test prep is as individually tailored as a private
tutor's attention, but backed by a system that remembers, adapts, and improves with
every session — starting in the communities the industry has overlooked, and
expanding into the infrastructure the whole tutoring industry runs on.

## Mission Statement

Charles River Tutors delivers personalized, data-informed SAT, ACT, and GRE
preparation across Greater Boston — starting with the Korean-American community in
Newton, Lexington, Brookline, and Cambridge — pairing elite tutors from Harvard, MIT,
and Boston's top universities with a curriculum that adapts to each student's
strengths and weaknesses after every session, not just at the start.

## Slogan

**"Prep That Learns You Back."**
